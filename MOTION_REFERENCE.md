# Allu Arjun motion reconstruction

Reference: the user-provided `WhatsApp Video 2026-09-28 at 2.37.46 PM.mp4`, 13.97 seconds, depicting alche.studio. Reviewed at four frames per second plus public site behavior/implementation. Original source is not included in this project.

The experience now uses a single fixed viewport and a continuous 14-unit scroll timeline rather than separate vertically stacked sections.

| Reference interval | Reconstructed behavior |
| --- | --- |
| 0–0.8 s | White AA branding with a bevelled, reflective AA sculpture; pointer parallax and subtle idle movement |
| 0.8–3.6 s | Six Allu Arjun image panels pass right to left in perspective; sculpture remains behind them and continues rotating |
| 3.6–4.2 s | Panels exit; sculpture turns and drops as a light background wipes upward |
| 4.2–4.9 s | Highlighted mission typography |
| 4.9–6.6 s | Iridescent mark rotates edge-on, then enlarges into a full-screen transition |
| 6.6–9.6 s | Oversized cinema typography and film panels entering/exiting through depth |
| 9.6–11.2 s | Portrait panel expands to full screen, then moves upward |
| 11.2–14 s | Sculpture returns, drafting grid appears, AA/name reveal, grid disappears |

`app/motion.ts` owns camera/object keyframes. `app/page.tsx` owns panel, text, background, and section transitions. `app/sculpture.tsx` renders the same unioned 3D geometry through WebGL when available or depth-sorted Canvas perspective projection when unavailable; the latter is not a static image or CSS-only substitute. The silhouette is generated by `scripts/build-aa-geometry.mjs` from the researched AA monogram.

The footage is a low-resolution perspective recording rather than original project assets. This is a reconstructed animation, not an asserted pixel-identical copy. Film/portrait sources are recorded in ASSET_SOURCES.json.


## Colour and video revision

- Increased AA extrusion from 0.43 to 0.70 world units; iridescent finish is the default, with cyan, violet and amber lighting.
- Added three projected 3D light rings, stronger pointer parallax, a perspective floor and deeper yaw/roll in the scroll-driven film orbit.
- Replaced four film still panels, the Pushpa feature and the full-screen portrait with native muted MP4 loops sourced from the promotional trailer. RAAKA and AA23 retain their film-specific posters and official YouTube announcements.
- Load only clips close to the current scene; pause loops in menus, trailer dialogs and background tabs. A persistent Loops toggle supports user control, and reduced-motion preferences disable autoplay.
- Poster fallbacks remain until actual video playback starts.
